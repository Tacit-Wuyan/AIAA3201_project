- `wild/sam3_propainter_inpainted.mp4`: SAM3 + ProPainter on the wild clip.
- `wild/sam3_diffueraser_inpainted.mp4`: SAM3 + DiffuEraser on the wild clip.
- `davis/part2_tennis_inpainted.mp4`: Part 2 final output on DAVIS tennis.
- `davis/part3_sam3_diffueraser_boat_inpainted.mp4`: Part 3 SAM3 + DiffuEraser on DAVIS boat.
- `davis/part3_sam3_propainter_tennis_inpainted.mp4`: Part 3 SAM3 + ProPainter on DAVIS tennis.

These videos are intentionally small and are meant for README-level qualitative inspection, not for full evaluation.
