# Part 1 / Part 2 Final Sample Results

This folder contains compact final result videos for the mandatory sample videos and the wild video.
Only representative videos are included to keep the GitHub repository lightweight.

## Contents

- `bmx_trees/part1_inpainted.mp4`: Part 1 restored bmx-trees video.
- `bmx_trees/part1_mask.mp4`: Part 1 mask visualization for bmx-trees.
- `bmx_trees/part2_inpainted.mp4`: Part 2 restored bmx-trees video.
- `bmx_trees/part2_mask.mp4`: Part 2 mask visualization for bmx-trees.
- `tennis/part1_inpainted.mp4`: Part 1 restored tennis video.
- `tennis/part1_mask.mp4`: Part 1 mask visualization for tennis.
- `tennis/part2_inpainted.mp4`: Part 2 restored tennis video.
- `tennis/part2_mask.mp4`: Part 2 mask visualization for tennis.
- `wild_gymnastics/part1_inpainted.mp4`: Part 1 restored wild video.
- `wild_gymnastics/part1_mask.mp4`: Part 1 mask visualization for wild video.
- `wild_gymnastics/part2_inpainted.mp4`: Part 2 restored wild video.
- `wild_gymnastics/part2_mask.mp4`: Part 2 mask visualization for wild video.
- `part12_results_summary.csv`: Final Part 1/2 JM/JR summary for bmx-trees and tennis.

Full frame-level masks and intermediate folders are intentionally not included; they can be regenerated by running the pipelines.
